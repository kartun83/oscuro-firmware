Cambios

Qué cambió, versión a versión.

Todo lo que un músico puede notar, desde la primera versión pública.

No es el historial completo del proyecto. Empieza en la 0.84, la primera versión pública, y recoge solo versiones publicadas: Viroid TI Rack, Retrowave, Phosphor, Tympanika, DAW Controller (con y sin Bluetooth) y Viroid TI Rack para FM-1. El registro de cambios es el historial del propio proyecto y se mantiene en inglés.

0.89

2026-11-20 · abarca 0.88.1 a 0.89.2 · 13 actualizaciones

Novedades 12

FM-1 (7)

  • Oscuro 0.89 is the next public release line after 0.88, for the SMK-37 (Viroid TI Rack, Retrowave, DAW Controller also with Bluetooth, Phosphor, Tympanika) and the FM-1 (Viroid TI Rack). It replaces 0.88, whose Retrowave build could mishandle presets
  • a saved Viroid patch keeps the arp (pattern, latched chord, steps and settings), on the FM-1 and on the SMK-37 Viroid builds
  • FM-1 SEQ PLAY and SEQ REC run the arp pattern (PLAY starts and stops it, REC records the keys you play as steps)
  • a saved Viroid patch keeps the arp (User pattern, latched chord, Steps layer and arp settings) on the SMK-37 Viroid builds too, as on the FM-1
  • on the FM-1 a saved Viroid patch keeps the arp (User pattern, latched chord, Steps layer and the arp settings), restored by Arp Recall
  • FM-1 SEQ PLAY and SEQ REC run the arp pattern. PLAY starts and stops the arp over the latched chord (or the last key played) with no key held, and says so when there is nothing to play. REC records the keys you play as the steps of the arp's User pattern; if a pattern exists it asks first: F#3 overwrites, F#5 overdubs. REC or PLAY ends the recording.
  • Viroid gets the PERFORM home page and a live mod-matrix indicator: coloured squares on every knob a matrix slot reaches and a strip showing how far it moves the value. Matrix slots can be muted (Oct- + pad on the SMK-37, the keys C#4 to C#5 on the FM-1) and the mutes are saved with the patch. FM-1: the arp STEPS window marks the selected step, KN2 sets its gate and tie, and holding Oct- shows how the open screen works.

Sintetizador Viroid (1)

  • Viroid gets the PERFORM home page and a live mod-matrix indicator (coloured squares on every knob a matrix slot reaches); matrix slots can be muted and the mutes are saved with the patch

Otros motores de síntesis (1)

  • Retrowave and Phosphor start in the engine you last switched to, so DX7 patches you stored are there after a restart.

Ajustes, editor y sistema (3)

  • make patches with an AI chat (one-file guide per synth, bank built from the AI's text in the installer), CZ-1 patch import, and a Support page
  • CZ-1 patches load in the installer: Casio CZ SysEx dumps, AI-guide banks, and three starter banks.
  • make your own patches with an AI chat. Each synth has a one-file guide (website, or guides/llm/ in the package): give it to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, say what you want, and load the bank it makes. The installer's Synth patches tab takes the file, or the AI's text pasted into the new Paste text from the AI box.

  • in test builds, hold Oct- while switching on to start the USB updater, or hold Oct+ to run without the second core; Globe still starts safely.

  • Settings -> System -> Save Delay (15 s default, 30 s, 1 min, Off): changed settings are written after that wait with no edit and a quiet spell, so a flash write no longer lands mid-note; HS Saws and HS Cuts moved to Settings -> Synthesizer

General (2)

  • ending the About screen during a USB update no longer calls into the screen library from the real-time path

General (1)

  • app updates no longer interrupt installation to request a patch backup; backup remains optional under Patch memory.
