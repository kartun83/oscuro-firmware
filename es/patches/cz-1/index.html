Lo que la IA no puede hacer

La guía pide a la IA que diga estas cosas al final de su respuesta. Toca el banco, dile a la IA qué cambiar y carga el nuevo.

End every answer that contains a bank with a short paragraph headed What I couldn't do, in your own words. It covers:

  • You cannot hear the sounds. The values are informed estimates from how the engine works, not tuned by ear; some tones will need adjusting on the keyboard.
  • Anything the player asked for that this engine cannot do, and what you made instead.
  • Values you had to guess, or tones you are least sure of.
  • Whether you built the file yourself, or the player has to paste your text into the installer. If the packer reported an error you could not fix, say which tone and what the error said.

Never claim that you listened to, played or tested the sounds.

For CZ-1 in particular: it is a phase-distortion synth in the spirit of the Casio CZ series, re-created in Oscuro, not a sample-exact copy of a Casio CZ. There is no filter and no effect inside a tone: brightness comes from the DCW envelope and the resonant waves. The home-screen knobs (Wave, Vibrate, Mode and the others) are performance controls, not part of a tone, so a bank cannot set them. The keyboard plays 4 to 8 voices depending on its Mode setting. Say so when it matters.

Cómo funciona (para curiosos)

Las herramientas y los parches iniciales que se mencionan (tools/ y patches/) vienen con los paquetes de firmware de Oscuro. Cada guía describe el firmware de la versión que figura al pie.

Estas guías solo están en inglés por ahora.

The CZ-1 engine is a phase-distortion synth in the spirit of the Casio CZ series, in the Retrowave and Phosphor builds. Its patches are Casio CZ tones: two lines, each with a wave and three eight-stage envelopes (DCO pitch, DCW brightness, DCA loudness). General matters (what a bank is, the patch memory) are in README.md next to this file.

Three ways to get CZ-1 patches

  1. The starter banks. patches/cz1/ holds three banks of 32 tones made for Oscuro: oscuro_cz_classic (basses, brass, electric pianos, organs, strings, bells), oscuro_cz_synth (leads, plucks, pads, sync-like and resonant sweeps) and oscuro_cz_fx (ring and noise tones, drums, textures, moving pads). The installer also lists them under Starter banks.
  2. Casio CZ dumps you already have. Choose the .syx file on the installer's Synth patches tab. CZ-1 tones keep their own name; a CZ-101 voice stores no name, so it is named after its file (Noisy Punch Bass.syx becomes Noisy Punc, a file of several voices gives Name 1, Name 2, ...).
  3. Your own, made with an AI chat. Give guides/llm/oscuro-cz-ai-guide.md to an AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, ...) and say what you want: "eight brassy CZ tones and two bells". It answers with a bank as JSON; choose the .json file or paste the answer into Paste text from the AI on the Synth patches tab. The installer checks every value and says which one is wrong.

The installer switches the keyboard to CZ-1 by itself. The memory holds 8 banks of 16 CZ-1 patches; a name is at most 10 characters.

What a tone stores and what it does not

  • A tone stores the two lines' waves, levels and envelopes, the line mode (1, 2, 1+1', 1+2'), octave, detune, Ring or Noise modulation and the vibrato. The JSON keys and their ranges are in the AI guide, with recipes.
  • The home-screen knobs (Wave, Level, Octave, Detune, Vibrate, Bend, Mode, Volume) are performance controls, not part of a tone.
  • CZ-1 is a re-creation in the spirit of the CZ series, not a sample-exact Casio: a dump plays close to, not identical with, the original instrument.
  • Envelope rates are steep: a full swing takes about 0.5 s at rate 50, 1.5 s at 40 and 5 s at 30 (99 is instant).

On the keyboard

  • Edit: K1 to K8 edit the macros (Wave, Level, Octave, Detune, Vibrate, Bend, Mode, Volume). Wave chooses a waveform (Saw, Square, Pulse, DblSine, SawPls, ResSaw, ResTri, ResTrp) or Tone, the waveforms stored in the patch.
  • Save to the patch memory: hold Oct - and press Right (Right again overwrites the stored patch, Left stores a copy).
  • Mode is CZ-ONLY (8 voices), RETRO (6, the default) or FULL (4). A tone that plays both lines (1+1', 1+2') uses two voices.
