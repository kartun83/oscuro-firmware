Patches for the CZ-1 engine

The CZ-1 engine is a phase-distortion synth in the spirit of the Casio CZ series. It has no patch import yet: the installer's Synth patches tab does not take CZ-1 files, and there is no bank format to write. General matters (what a bank is, the patch memory) are in README.md next to this file.

En las versiones: Retrowave, Phosphor

Las herramientas y los parches iniciales que se mencionan (tools/ y patches/) vienen con los paquetes de firmware de Oscuro. Cada guía describe el firmware de la versión que figura al pie.

Estas guías solo están en inglés por ahora.

What you can do today

  • Edit on the keyboard. K1 to K8 edit the macros (Wave, Level, Octave, Detune, Vibrate, Bend, Mode, Volume). Wave chooses a waveform (Saw, Square, Pulse, DblSine, SawPls, ResSaw, ResTri, ResTrp) or Tone, the waveforms stored in the patch.
  • Save to the patch memory: hold Oct - and press Right (Right again overwrites the stored patch, Left stores a copy). The memory has 8 banks of 16 patches for CZ-1.
  • Mode is CZ-ONLY (8 voices), RETRO (6, the default) or FULL (4).

Later

An import route for CZ-1 patches (.oscbank, and perhaps the CZ SysEx dumps) is on the list; when it exists, this guide will describe it and the package will carry starter patches.
