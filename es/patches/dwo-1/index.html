Making your own DWO-1 patches

DWO-1 is the three-oscillator synth of the Phosphor build: three digital-wave oscillators with a level stage each, the filter, a final volume stage, four envelopes, three LFOs, sync, amplitude modulation and a modulation matrix. A patch is 143 numbers (16 macros, 61 EDIT values, an 8-slot matrix of 56 numbers, the name). You describe patches in a small JSON file, a tool turns the file into a bank, and the installer sends the bank to the keyboard. You need rw_banks.py (in tools/, next to rw_schema.json) and Python 3. The knob layout is in ../PHOSPHOR.md; general matters are in README.md next to this file.

En las versiones: Phosphor

The three steps

  1. Write my_dwo.json (format below). Start from a factory set: patches/dwo1/dwo1_showcase.json is the same format.
  2. Build the bank; the tool checks every key, source and destination, and that every value is 0 to 127, and tells you which one is wrong (it does not check each key's own range: keep to the table, the keyboard limits a higher value to the top of the range):
python3 tools/rw_banks.py build my_dwo.json my_dwo.oscbank --name "My DWO-1"
  1. Open the installer page, Synth patches, choose my_dwo.oscbank, send. The installer switches the keyboard to DWO-1 by itself; to switch by hand, hold Oct -, press PATCH, turn K1 to DWO-1 and press >. python3 tools/rw_banks.py list my_dwo.json lists the names and python3 tools/rw_banks.py keys es prints every key with its INIT value, then the matrix sources and destinations.

The JSON file

An object with "engine": "es", an optional "bank" name, and a "patches" list (at most 128). Each patch has a name (up to 10 ASCII characters), any of the keys below (a key you leave out keeps the INIT value) and an optional mx list for the matrix. cat and desc are notes for people.

{
  "engine": "es",
  "bank": "My DWO-1",
  "patches": [
    {"name": "MY LEAD", "ES_P_W1": 0, "ES_P_W2": 7, "ES_E_O2_LVL": 55, "ES_P_DETUNE": 40, "ES_P_CUTOFF": 96,
     "ES_P_VIB": 36, "ES_E_LA_DELAY": 35},
    {"name": "MY ZAP", "ES_P_W1": 0, "ES_P_W2": 0, "ES_E_SYNC": 1, "ES_E_O1_LVL": 0, "ES_E_O2_LVL": 63,
     "ES_E_E2_L1": 126, "ES_E_E2_L2": 84, "ES_E_E2_L3": 63, "ES_E_E2_T1": 10, "ES_E_E2_T2": 20,
     "mx": [["ENV2", [["O2PIT", 63], ["O2PIT", 63]]], ["WHEEL", [["CUTOFF", 30]]]]}
  ]
}

The matrix (mx) is a list of up to 8 slots in order. A slot is ["SOURCE", [["DESTINATION", amount], ...]] with up to three destinations and amounts from -64 to +63. Several slots may feed the same destination and their amounts add. A source at its maximum with amount 63 moves the destination by the full amount in the tables below.

How the sound is made

Oscillators and level stages. Three oscillators play waves from a bank of 32 (list below). Each has its own octave, semitone and fine tuning and a level stage (ES_E_O1_LVL to ES_E_O3_LVL). The level stages are the mixer; the matrix can open and close them (destinations DCA1 to DCA3). Sync makes oscillator 1 restart oscillator 2 on every cycle; AM makes oscillator 1 modulate the amplitude of oscillator 2: the product plays at level stage 1's level (ES_E_O1_LVL and DCA1), level stage 2 is not used, and oscillator 3 plays on its own.

Envelopes. Envelopes 1 to 3 are free: three levels (63 is zero, 126 is +1, 0 is -1) reached in three times, then held on level 3 until the key is released, then back to zero in the release time. Envelope 1 goes to the cutoff (depth: Flt Env, ES_P_FENV); 2 and 3 do nothing until you route them in the matrix. Envelope 4 is the volume: attack (ES_P_ATTACK) to the peak, the Hold time to level 2, decay (ES_P_DECAY) to the sustain level (ES_P_SUSTAIN), release.

LFOs. Three, each with a shape, a depth at the start of the note, a delay and a depth after the delay (so a vibrato can fade in). LFO 1 is wired to pitch (Vibrato) and LFO 2 to the cutoff (LFO2 Cut); LFO 3 only works through the matrix. A noise LFO holds a random value for each cycle (sample and hold).

LFO rates (ES_P_L1RATE, ES_P_L2RATE, ES_E_LC_RATE, 0 to 127): 0 is 0.1 Hz, 40 about 0.5 Hz, 60 about 1.0 Hz, 80 about 2.1 Hz, 100 about 4.6 Hz, 127 about 12.8 Hz. LFO shapes: 0 triangle, 1 falling saw, 2 square, 3 noise.

Cutoff: ES_P_CUTOFF 0 is 20 Hz, 127 is 20 kHz. Resonance goes from 0 to 31.

Recipes

  • Hard-sync lead: ES_E_SYNC 1, oscillator 1 level 0, oscillator 2 level 63, envelope 2 to O2PIT twice at 63 (24 semitones) with a short first time.
  • Organ: waves 16, 17, 18, volume sustain 63, attack 0, release 2, a little vibrato.
  • Electric piano: a pluck or marimba wave plus a sine an octave up; envelope 3 to DCA2 for the bright strike, velocity to DCA2.
  • Pad: strings and saw waves, detune 30, attack around 36, LFO 2 to the cutoff, KEY and WHEEL to CUTOFF.
  • Gate: LFO 3 square to DCA1 to DCA3 with base levels around 40 chops the sound at the LFO rate.
  • Bubbles: LFO 3 noise to O1PIT to O3PIT at 22 makes random pitches.
  • Acid: ES_E_VOICES 1, filter cutoff 45, resonance 24, envelope 1 fast and deep, velocity on envelope 1 as the accent.

Asking an LLM

Paste the key list (python3 tools/rw_banks.py keys es), the "How the sound is made", "Scales" and matrix sections above, and ask for a JSON file in the format shown. Tell it to use only those keys, sources and destinations, whole numbers inside the ranges, names of at most 10 characters, and to list only what differs from INIT. Build the file with the tool: it names every key or value it refuses.
