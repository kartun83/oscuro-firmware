Un archivo por sintetizador, escrito para ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini o cualquier otro chat de IA. Sin herramientas ni Python: cópialo, di lo que quieres y carga lo que recibas.

Lo que la IA no puede hacer

La guía pide a la IA que diga estas cosas al final de su respuesta. Toca el banco, dile a la IA qué cambiar y carga el nuevo.

End every answer that contains a bank with a short paragraph headed What I couldn't do, in your own words. It covers:

  • You cannot hear the sounds. The values are informed estimates from how the engine works, not tuned by ear; some kits will need adjusting on the keyboard.
  • Anything the player asked for that this engine cannot do, and what you made instead.
  • Values you had to guess, or kits you are least sure of.
  • Whether you built the file yourself, or the player has to paste your text into the installer. If the packer reported an error you could not fix, say which kit and what the error said.

Never claim that you listened to, played or tested the sounds.

For MT drums in particular: every sound is synthesized from one oscillator and one noise source (no samples, so no real acoustic recordings, breaks or vocal shots), there are 8 channels per kit, and there are no effects such as reverb or delay. Say so when a request needs them.

Cómo funciona (para curiosos)

Las herramientas y los parches iniciales que se mencionan (tools/ y patches/) vienen con los paquetes de firmware de Oscuro. Cada guía describe el firmware de la versión que figura al pie.

Estas guías solo están en inglés por ahora.

MT DRUMS is a Microtonic-style drum synth: eight channels, each with two stored sounds (L and R, 25 settings each) and a Morph that blends L into R. One patch is a whole kit (416 bytes). Notes 36 to 43 play the eight channels at the kit's morph position; notes 44 to 51 play the same channels pinned to side R. General matters (what a bank is, the patch memory, using an LLM) are in README.md next to this file. The factory kit banks are in patches/mt/: a jungle set and genre sets (house, electro, drum and bass, psy trance, ...).

Kits are described in numbers. ../llm-guides/oscuro-mt-ai-guide.md is a complete guide written for an AI chat: the 25 parameters with the formulas between values and Hz, milliseconds and dB, recipes per genre, and a packer that turns the answer into a valid bank (or paste the answer into the installer). Read it yourself too: it is the best reference.

Then open the installer page, Synth patches, choose the .oscbank and send. A bank holds 16 kits. In the Retrowave and Phosphor builds the installer switches the keyboard to the engine of the file by itself; to switch by hand, hold Oct -, press PATCH, turn K1 to MT DRUMS and press >.

The kit

PartMeaning
8 channelsusual roles: 1 kick, 2 snare, 3 closed hat, 4 open hat, 5 perc or rim, 6 perc or tom, 7 clap or ride, 8 crash or FX
2 sidesL and R each hold all 25 parameters of every channel; the Morph (0 = all L, 255 = all R) blends them
nameup to 10 ASCII characters

The 25 parameters of a channel
