13 Clips on the pads

Launch and record clips from the keyboard. The Clips action turns pad bank B into a small clip launcher: two scenes of eight tracks, with a menu of clip actions on a held pad.

Switching it on

Hold Oct- (or the Shift pad) and tap pad 14 of bank B. Both pad rows now show clips; choose another action (Oct- + pad 9-13) to go back to the track pads.

What the pads show

  • Top row: the clips of the eight tracks in the selected scene.
  • Bottom row: the same tracks in the next scene.
  • Each pad is lit in its clip's colour; empty slots stay dark.

Launching and recording

  • Tap a pad to launch its clip. The launch happens when you let go, so a hold can open the menu instead.
  • Tapping an empty slot on an armed track records a new clip there; on an unarmed track the screen says Empty slot (arm the track to record).
  • Oct- + pad 15 stops all clips. Oct- + pads 3 / 4 move the selected scene, Oct- + pad 8 launches it.

Clip actions: hold a pad

Hold a clip pad for half a second. The Browser view opens with a list of actions for that slot:

ActionDoes
Record 4 barsArms the track if needed and records a 4-bar clip into the empty slot
Record 8 barsThe same, 8 bars
DuplicateDuplicates the clip into the slot below
QuantizeQuantizes the clip to Live's record quantization (1/16 when that is off)
Double loopDoubles the clip's loop and its notes (MIDI clips only)
Halve loopHalves the clip's loop
DeleteDeletes the clip: press Right twice (the screen asks Delete clip? Right again)

Turn K1 to pick an action, Right does it, Left closes the list. The status bar reads Clip and Right: do it Left: close. Recording into a slot that already has a clip, or on a track that cannot record, gives a short message instead.

Capture MIDI

Played something good without recording? Oct- + pad 5 of bank B runs Live's Capture MIDI. If there is nothing to capture, the screen says so.

Good to know

  • The clip launcher pads are tested on a computer only, not yet in Live with a keyboard.
