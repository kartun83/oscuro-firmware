9 Drum pads, steps and key lanes

Program a beat from the keyboard. With a Drum Rack on the track, pad bank B shows its drum pads: pick a sound, hold its pad, and the sixteen pads become sixteen steps of that sound in the clip. Play on pad bank A, program on pad bank B.

When the pads become drum pads

In the Device view, pad bank B turns into drum pads when the selected device is a Drum Rack, or when the selected device has no switches for the device pads and the track has a Drum Rack. The screen says Pads: <device> drum pads (hold one: steps) once.

The drum pads only select and program; they make no sound. Play the drums on pad bank A.

The drum pads page

PadsDo
Top row, 1-8Eight drum pads from C1, lit in their colours; the selected one bright. Tap selects it in Live. Hold half a second: its steps
Bottom row, 9-16Mute of the same eight pads, or Solo when the bottom-row action is Solo
Oct- + Left / RightThe previous / next eight pads
Left / RightDevice banks, as in the Device view

The steps page

All sixteen pads are steps: sixteen sixteenth notes of that pad in the clip of the selected track and scene. If there is no clip, a one-bar clip is made. Lit = a note, dim = empty, white = the step playing now. The status bar shows the pad and Steps 1-16/16.

ControlDoes
Press an empty stepAdd a note
Press a note and let goRemove it (unless you turned a knob while holding it)
Hold a step, turn K1Velocity
Hold a step, turn K2Length
Hold a step, turn K3Probability
Hold a step, turn K4Velocity range (Live's random velocity spread)
Hold a step, turn K5Mute the note (right = muted)
Hold a step, turn K6Release velocity
Left / RightPrevious / next sixteen steps
Oct- + Left / RightPrevious / next drum pad
Shift pad + LeftBack to the drum pads

K7 and K8 stay blank while a step is held; Oct- + K8 still sets the tempo.

Key lanes: steps for any MIDI track

On a MIDI track with the track pads showing, Shift pad + Right opens the same steps page for one key, C3 the first time and the last key you used after that. The screen says Steps: <track> key C3 (Oct- + Left/Right: key) once.

  • Steps light in the track's colour; edit them and K1-K6 as above.
  • Oct- + Left / Right moves the key a semitone; Left / Right page the steps.
  • Shift pad + Left, another track, or another view closes it. A selected Drum Rack keeps the drum pads.

Building a beat

  1. Select the Drum Rack and switch to pad bank B (Knob Bank + Fader Bank).
  2. Hold the kick's pad (C1). Press steps 1, 5, 9 and 13.
  3. Oct- + Right twice to the snare (D1); press 5 and 13.
  4. Oct- + Right four more times to the closed hi-hat (F#1); press every other step, hold one and turn K3 down so it only sometimes plays.
  5. SEQ PLAY. Shift pad + Left for the drum pads, and mute sounds on the bottom row.

Good to know

  • Without a Shift pad, leave the Device view and come back to leave the steps page. Key lanes need a Shift pad.
  • Drum pads, steps and key lanes are tested on a computer only, not yet in Live with a keyboard.
