6 Controls that work in every view

Some controls do the same thing whatever the screen shows: the transport, the view button, the tracks on pad bank B and the second fader bank. Learn these once and they work everywhere.

Oct- is the modifier

The SMK-37 has no Shift button, so Oct- held is the modifier. A chord with Oct- never shifts the octave; a lone tap still steps it down when you let go.

The Shift pad works the same way. Make one pad a Shift key in Globe → Physical Controls → Pads → Shift Pad. It gives 0.1 BPM steps with Oct- + K8, lights the choice of pad actions while held, opens key lanes, and leaves the steps page. Held while turning a knob, Oct- or the Shift pad gives fine steps.

Tracks on pad bank B

Press Knob Bank and Fader Bank together to switch the pads between bank A and bank B. Bank A plays notes (a Drum Rack, instruments, the Harmony Helper); bank B controls tracks.

  • Top row, pads 1-8: select a track, lit in its colour; the selected track is brightest.
  • Bottom row, pads 9-16: one action on the same eight tracks:
Pad to choose itActionLit
9Muteyellow, bright when muted
10Soloblue, bright when soloed
11Armred, bright when armed
12Stop the track's clipsgrey, pulses while a clip plays
13Launch the clip in the selected scenethe clip's colour
14Clips: both rows become an 8 × 2 clip launchersee Clips on the pads

To choose, hold Oct- (or the Shift pad) and tap pad 9-14. Only while you hold the Shift pad do the pads light up as that menu. Two more bottom-row chords: Oct- + pad 15 stops all clips, Oct- + pad 16 switches between Session and Arrangement view.

The top row with Oct- (or the Shift pad):

In the Device view the pads may become device switches, drum pads, steps, slices, Looper or rack-variation pads instead; see the Device view chapters. The Mixer and Overview always keep the track pads.

Faders

  • Fader Bank A: faders 1-4 are the volumes of four tracks, the fader window (F1-F4 in the Mixer view).
  • Fader Bank B: faders 1-4 are the selected track's Volume, Pan, Send A and Send B.

Faders use soft takeover: a fader waits until you move it across the value, then takes over; a message says which way. soft_takeover: False in user_settings.py switches this off.

Knob Bank B

Knob Bank switches the knobs between K1-K8 and K9-K16. What K9-K16 do depends on the view.

Recording automation

Turning a knob or moving a fader opens an automation gesture in Live, as grabbing it with the mouse would. With Arrangement record or Session automation recording on, Live writes what you move, and only that.
