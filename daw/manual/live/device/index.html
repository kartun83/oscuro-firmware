Getting there

The Device view is where every session starts. From another view, tap SEQ until the left of the status bar shows the device's name.

What the screen shows

  • Status bar: the device's name on the left; the bank's position and name in the middle ("2/5 Filter").
  • Eight knob cells for K1-K8: name, value, arc. An empty cell has no parameter in this bank.
  • The selected track along the bottom: its colour, its name, and M, S, R lit when on.

Banks: what the knobs reach

Live has no remote pages like some DAWs; the script makes banks of eight parameters:

  • for Live's own devices, Live's best-of banks (the parameters Push shows);
  • for a Max for Live device, the device's own banks;
  • for an Instrument or Effect Rack, its macros;
  • for anything else, the parameters in order, eight at a time.

The status bar names the bank. A device without parameters shows no parameters; on a track without devices the status bar reads No device and hold SEQ: browse.

Device switches on the pads

When the selected device has on/off parameters in the current bank or the next, pad bank B toggles them instead of the tracks: top row = this bank, bottom row = the next. A switch is lit in the script's amber, bright when on; other pads stay dark. The status bar adds | pads and the screen says Pads: <device> switches once. Tapping a switch writes automation like a knob. The Oct- and Shift pad chords keep their meaning.

Other devices give the pads other jobs: a Drum Rack (Drum pads, steps and key lanes), Simpler in Slicing mode, a Looper and racks with variations (Slices, Looper and rack variations).

How fine is a knob?

A knob click moves a parameter by 1/128 of its range, 1/1024 with Oct- or the Shift pad held. fine: True in user_settings.py makes the normal step 1/256.

Good to know

  • Live has no modulation readout for scripts, so the knobs show no modulation tick.
  • The knobs follow Live's selection: click a device in Live and the screen follows it.
