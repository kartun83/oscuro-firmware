Shaping a sound

Device view.

  1. Select the track: pad bank B, top row. The bottom of the screen shows its name and colour.
  2. K1-K8 are the first bank of the selected device; Right for the next bank, Left back.
  3. Oct- + Right steps to the next device on the track.
  4. Hold Oct- or the Shift pad while turning for exact values.
  5. Switch Knob Bank to B: K9-K16 are the track's volume, pan and sends A-F, whichever device is selected.

For a rack, the bank is its eight macros. Store good macro settings as variations and recall them from the pads (Slices, Looper and rack variations).

Mixing

Mixer view. SEQ to Mixer; faders 1-4 on the strips marked F1-F4, Right for the next four. K1-K8 pan, K9-K16 Send A. Oct- + pad 10 to solo tracks from the bottom row. For one track in detail, select it and switch Fader Bank to B.

Recording with automation

  1. SEQ to Overview. Set the loop with K4 / K5 and the metronome with Oct- + SEQ.
  2. Arm the track: Oct- + pad 11 (Arm), then the track's bottom-row pad.
  3. SEQ back to Device.
  4. SEQ REC starts Arrangement recording. Play, and turn knobs where you want movement: each turn writes automation for that parameter only.
  5. SEQ PLAY stops; Oct- + SEQ PLAY stops and returns to the start.

In the Session view use Oct- + SEQ REC (Session record) and the Clips pads: tap an empty slot on the armed track, or hold it and choose Record 4 bars (Clips on the pads).

Jamming with clips

  1. Oct- + pad 14: the pads are the clips of two scenes.
  2. Tap clips to launch them; Oct- + pads 3 / 4 move the scenes, Oct- + pad 8 launches a whole scene.
  3. Played a good idea without recording? Oct- + pad 5: Capture MIDI.
  4. Hold a clip for Duplicate, Double loop or Quantize.

Loading sounds

Browser. Hold SEQ for the selected device's presets; turn K1 and press Right to hear each one in place. Oct- + hold SEQ for the whole browser, to add an effect after the device.

Programming a beat

Device view, drum track. Select the Drum Rack, switch to pad bank B, hold a drum pad and press steps; hold a step and turn K1-K6. See Drum pads, steps and key lanes.

Navigating a big set

  • Tracks by eights: Oct- + pad 1 / 2 (any view), or Oct- + Left / Right in the Mixer.
  • Scenes: Oct- + pad 3 / 4.
  • Session or Arrangement: Oct- + pad 16.
  • Through time: in the Overview, Left / Right a bar, Oct- + Left / Right four bars, K6 the play position.
  • Undo / Redo: Oct- + pad 6 / 7.
