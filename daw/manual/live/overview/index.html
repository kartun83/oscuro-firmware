Getting there

Tap SEQ until the status bar reads Overview. The middle of the status bar names Live's main view: Session or Arrangement.

What the screen shows

  • Tempo in large digits and the position as bar.beat.sixteenth, with the time signature and PLAY, REC or STOP.
  • Chips, lit while on: LOOP, MET, OVR, AUTO, PUNCH.
  • Knob cells for the knobs.

Live gives scripts no project name, so none is shown.

The controls

ControlIn the Overview
K1Tempo: 1 BPM per click (0.1 with Oct- or the Shift pad)
K2Swing amount
K3Global groove amount
K4Loop start, one bar per click (a beat with the modifier)
K5Loop length, one bar per click (a beat with the modifier)
K6Play position, one bar per click (a beat with the modifier)
K7Master volume
K8Cue (preview) volume
K9-K16 (Knob Bank B)Volumes of the return tracks A-H
Left / RightPlay position one bar back / forward
Oct- + Left / RightFour bars back / forward

Oct- + K8 is the tempo here too, as in every view.

Good to know

  • Pads, faders and transport work as everywhere.
  • Oct- + pad 16 of bank B switches between Session and Arrangement; the status bar follows.
