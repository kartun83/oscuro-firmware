6 Controls that work in every view

Some controls do the same thing whatever the screen shows: the transport, the view button, the tracks on pad bank B and the second fader bank. Learn these once and they work everywhere.

Oct- is the modifier

The SMK-37 has no Shift button, so Oct- held is the modifier for everything the script does. A chord with Oct- never shifts the octave: a lone tap of Oct- still steps the octave down when you let go, but once you used Oct- together with another control, its release does nothing.

The Shift pad works the same way for the script. Make one pad a Shift key in Globe → Physical Controls → Pads → Shift Pad. It is optional; everything has an Oct- chord too. Two things only the Shift pad does:

  • held while turning Oct- + K8, it gives 0.1 BPM steps;
  • held, it lights the bottom pad row as the choice of pad actions (below).

Held while turning a knob, either one gives fine steps (eight times finer than normal).

Tracks on pad bank B

Press Knob Bank and Fader Bank together to switch the pads between bank A and bank B. Bank A plays notes (and the Harmony Helper when it is on); bank B controls tracks, so you can switch back and forth without losing either.

Exceptions in the Device view. When the selected device has on/off switches, pad bank B toggles those instead of the tracks (see Device view). On a drum track it becomes the drum pads and the step sequencer (see Drum pads and steps). The Mixer and Overview always keep the track pads.

  • Top row, pads 1-8: select a track. Each pad is lit in its track's colour; the selected track is brightest. Unlit pads have no track.
  • Bottom row, pads 9-16: one action on the same eight tracks. Which action is up to you:
Pad to choose itActionLit
9Muteyellow, bright when muted
10Soloblue, bright when soloed
11Armred, bright when armed
12Stop the track's clipgrey, pulses while a clip plays, blinks when a stop is queued
13Launch the clip in the selected scenethe clip's colour; dim = stopped, pulse = queued, blink = recording

To choose, hold Oct- (or the Shift pad) and tap pad 9-13. The choice stays until you change it. Only while you hold the Shift pad do pads 9-13 light up as the menu, with the current action brightest.

Faders

  • Fader Bank A (normal): faders 1-4 are the volumes of four tracks, the fader window. In the Mixer view the window is marked F1-F4; Left / Right move it there.
  • Fader Bank B: faders 1-4 are the selected track's Volume, Pan, Send 1 and Send 2.

Faders use soft takeover: a fader that does not sit where the value is waits until you move it across the value, then takes over. A message tells you which way to move it. Fader pick-up → Jump in the script preferences switches this off. Details in Mixer view.

Knob Bank B

Knob Bank switches the knobs between bank A (K1-K8) and bank B (K9-K16). What K9-K16 do depends on the view. The screen shows the bank you are on.

Recording automation by touch

Turning a knob or moving a fader touches its parameter in Bitwig, and releases it half a second after you stop. With Bitwig's automation write mode set to Touch, you can record automation by simply playing the knobs: only what you move is written. See Workflows.
