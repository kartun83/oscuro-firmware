9 Drum pads, steps and key lanes

Program a beat from the keyboard. With a Drum Machine on the track, pad bank B shows its drum pads: pick a sound, hold its pad, and the sixteen pads become sixteen steps of that sound in the clip. Play on pad bank A, program on pad bank B.

When the pads become drum pads

This works in the Device view. Pad bank B turns into drum pads when:

  1. the selected device is a Drum Machine (or another device with drum pads), or
  2. the selected device has no on/off switches for the device pads (see Device view), and the track's first instrument is a Drum Machine.

So on a drum track, selecting the Drum Machine, or an effect without switches, brings up the drum pads; selecting a device with switches gives the device pads instead. The screen says Pads: <device> drum pads (hold one: steps) once. In the Mixer and Overview, pad bank B is always the track pads.

The drum pads only select and program; they make no sound. Play the drums on pad bank A (or the keys) as usual.

The drum pads page

PadsDo
Top row, 1-8Eight drum pads, starting at C1, lit in their colours; the selected one is bright. Tap selects the pad in Bitwig. Hold half a second: its steps
Bottom row, 9-16Mute of the same eight drum pads (yellow, bright = muted). Solo (blue) instead when the bottom-row action is Solo
Oct- + Left / RightThe next / previous eight drum pads (C1-G1, G#1-D#2, …)
Left / RightRemote pages, as in the Device view

The status bar shows the Drum Machine's name and the window ("C1-G1"). The Oct- and Shift pad chords on the pads keep their meaning: for example Oct- + pad 10 makes the bottom row solo the drum pads.

The steps page

Hold a drum pad for half a second and all sixteen pads become steps: sixteen sixteenth notes of that drum sound in the clip shown in Bitwig's editor.

  • Lit in the pad's colour: a note on that step. Dim: empty. White: the step playing now.
  • No clip yet? The first press makes a one-bar clip in the track's launcher slot and puts the note in it. If no clip appears within two seconds (no slot to make it in), the screen says No clip to write to: select a slot: select an empty launcher slot in Bitwig and press again.
  • The status bar shows the drum pad's name and Steps 1-16/16 (which steps, of how many in the clip's loop).
ControlDoes
Press an empty stepAdd a note (velocity 100, one sixteenth long)
Press a note and let goRemove it
Hold a step and turn K1Velocity
Hold a step and turn K2Length, a sixteenth per click
Hold a step and turn K3Chance: how often the note plays
Hold a step and turn K4Repeats: ratchets within the note
Hold a step and turn K5Occurrence: when the note plays (Always, First, Not first, Prev, Not prev, Prev channel, Not prev channel, Prev key, Not prev key, Fill, Not fill), one per click
Hold a step and turn K6Recurrence: Off, or play only on the first of 2 to 8 cycles of the clip
Hold a step and turn K7Timbre (for instruments that follow per-note timbre)
Hold a step and turn K8Pressure (per-note pressure). Oct- + K8 still sets the tempo
Left / RightThe previous / next sixteen steps (in a longer clip)
Oct- + Left / RightThe previous / next drum sound, staying on the steps page
Shift pad + LeftBack to the drum pads page

A note you held and edited with a knob stays when you let go. While a step is held, the eight knob cells show its Velocity, Length, Chance, Repeats, Occurrence, Recurrence, Timbre and Pressure; hold Oct- or the Shift pad for fine steps.

Building a beat

  1. Put a Drum Machine on a track and select it. Switch to pad bank B (Knob Bank + Fader Bank).
  2. Hold the kick's pad (C1, pad 1). Press steps 1, 5, 9 and 13.
  3. Oct- + Right twice to the snare (D1). Press 5 and 13.
  4. Oct- + Right four more times to the closed hi-hat (F#1). Press every other step; hold one and turn K1 down for a softer hit, K3 down so it only sometimes plays.
  5. SEQ PLAY: the white step runs over the pattern. Shift pad + Left to go back to the drum pads and mute sounds on the bottom row.

Good to know

  • On the drum pads page Oct- + Left / Right moves the drum pad window, not the device chain. To change device, select it in Bitwig or leave the drum pads (tap SEQ around the views).
  • Without a Shift pad, the way back from the steps page is to leave the Device view and come back: tap SEQ three times. Setting a Shift pad (Globe → Physical Controls → Pads → Shift Pad) makes it one press.
  • The steps edit the launcher clip in the editor, not the arranger.
  • Drum pads, steps and key lanes are new and tested on a computer only: they have not yet been tried in Bitwig with a keyboard.

Steps for any instrument: key lanes

The steps page also works on a melodic track, one key at a time. In the Device view, on a track that holds notes, with the track pads showing (no switches, no Drum Machine):

  1. Shift pad + Right opens the steps of one key, C3 the first time. The screen says Steps: <track> key C3 (Oct- + Left/Right: key) once; the status bar shows the note name and Steps 1-16/16.
  2. Edit the steps exactly as with drums: press to add, press and let go to remove, hold and turn K1-K8. Notes are lit in the track's colour.
  3. Oct- + Left / Right moves to the key a semitone lower / higher. Left / Right page through a longer clip.
  4. Shift pad + Left closes it. Changing track or leaving the Device view closes it too. The next time it opens on the key you used last.

Key lanes need a Shift pad (Globe → Physical Controls → Pads → Shift Pad). A selected Drum Machine keeps the drum pads instead.
