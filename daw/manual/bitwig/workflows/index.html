Shaping a sound

Device view. You want to play a part and adjust its synth while you play.

  1. Select the track: pad bank B, top row (or click it in Bitwig). The bottom of the screen shows its name and colour.
  2. The knobs are the first remote page of the selected device. Play with one hand, turn with the other.
  3. Right for the next page (the status bar shows "2/5 Filter"), Left to go back.
  4. Oct- + Right steps to the next device in the chain: an EQ, a delay. The knobs follow.
  5. For an exact value, hold Oct- or the Shift pad while you turn: steps become eight times finer.
  6. Switch Knob Bank to B for the track's own macros (K9-K16), which stay the same whichever device you select.

A modulation tick on a knob arc shows where an LFO or envelope moves that parameter, so you see whether your knob is the base value under a moving modulation.

Mixing

Mixer view. You want to balance eight tracks.

  1. Tap SEQ to Mixer. Faders 1-4 are the strips marked F1-F4.
  2. Pull each fader through its track's level; the ^ / v mark next to F1-F4 goes away when it picks up.
  3. Right moves the faders to tracks 5-8, then on to the next eight tracks.
  4. K1-K8 pan, K9-K16 (Knob Bank B) send to send 1. Watch Bitwig's mixer for the values.
  5. On pad bank B, choose Solo (hold Oct-, tap pad 10) and solo tracks one by one; choose Mute (pad 9) to take parts out.
  6. For one track in detail, select it and switch Fader Bank to B: volume, pan, send 1, send 2 on the faders.

Recording with automation

Overview and Device views. You want to record a take and the knob moves with it.

  1. In Bitwig set automation write mode to Touch and enable automation write (the AUTO chip in the Overview lights).
  2. Tap SEQ to Overview. Set the loop with K12 / K13, the metronome with Oct- + SEQ, and its level with K11.
  3. Arm the track: pad bank B, Arm action (hold Oct-, tap pad 11), then the track's bottom-row pad.
  4. Go back to where the knobs you want are: tap SEQ until Device.
  5. SEQ REC starts recording. Play, and turn knobs where you want movement. Each knob writes only while you turn it and lets go half a second after you stop, so the rest of the automation stays.
  6. SEQ PLAY stops. Oct- + SEQ PLAY stops and returns to the start for the next take.

For clip launcher takes use Oct- + SEQ REC (launcher overdub) and the Launch pad action.

Loading devices from the browser

Browser view. You want a new sound on a track.

  1. Select the track. To replace a device, select it in the Device view (Oct- + Left / Right).
  2. Hold SEQ. K2 chooses the category, K3 the creator, K4 the file type.
  3. Turn K1 and play: Bitwig previews each result.
  4. Right loads it, Left gives up. You are back in the view you came from, and the knobs show the new device.

On a track without a device, holding SEQ adds one. To add an effect after the selected device instead of replacing it, hold Oct- while you hold SEQ.

Programming a beat

Device view, drum track. Select the Drum Machine, switch to pad bank B, hold a drum sound's pad and press steps; hold a step and turn K1-K4 for velocity, length, chance and repeats. The whole walk-through is in Drum pads and steps.

Navigating a big project

You want to reach track 23 or scene 12 without the mouse.

  • Tracks by eights: hold Oct- and tap pad 1 or 2 of bank B's top row (any view), or Oct- + Left / Right in the Mixer. The Mixer's status bar shows which eight ("Tracks 17-24").
  • Then select with a top-row pad. The knobs, the bottom of the screen and Fader Bank B follow.
  • Scenes: hold Oct-, tap pad 3 / 4 to move the scene; pad 8 launches the whole scene; the Launch action launches single clips of it.
  • Through time: in the Overview, Left / Right step a bar, Oct- + Left / Right four bars, K14 moves the play position.
  • Through a chain: in the Device view, Oct- + Left / Right.
