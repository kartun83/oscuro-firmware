Getting there

Hold SEQ for half a second in any view. Bitwig opens its pop-up browser to replace the selected device, or to add a device when the track has none. Hold Oct- and hold SEQ to insert a new device after the selected one instead. The keyboard shows the Browser view.

What the screen shows

  • The browser's title (what is being browsed; No results when a filter leaves nothing) and the cursor's position in the results ("41/412").
  • Eight result rows; the cursor's row is filled with the accent colour. Long names are cut.
  • The knob labels along the bottom: Result, Category, Creator, Type, and < Cancel / OK > over Left / Right.

The controls

ControlIn the Browser
K1Scroll the results. Bitwig previews the result under the cursor; play the keys to hear it
K2Category filter
K3Creator filter
K4File type filter
RightOK: load the result and close
LeftCancel: close without loading
SEQ (tap)Cancel, as Left

After closing, the keyboard returns to the view you came from. Closing the browser in Bitwig (Esc, a click outside) does the same.

Finding a sound

  1. Select the track and the device to replace (Device view: Oct- + Left / Right).
  2. Hold SEQ. Turn K2 to the category you want ("Bass"), K3 to narrow by creator if you like.
  3. Turn K1 slowly and play a few notes on each result.
  4. Right keeps it, Left keeps the old device.

Good to know

  • SEQ alone replaces the selected device; Oct- + SEQ held adds a new one after it, so you can build a chain (synth, then EQ, then delay) from the keyboard.
  • The rows follow the cursor a page of eight at a time.
