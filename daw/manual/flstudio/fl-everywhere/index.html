6 Controls that work in every view

These controls do the same in the Device, Mixer and Overview views. Oct- held is the modifier; the Shift pad (if you set one) works the same, and also turns pages.

Faders

ControlDoes
Faders 1-4Volumes of the 4 mixer inserts in the fader window (F1-F4 on the Mixer screen)
Fader Bank BThe selected channel's volume and pan, then its insert's volume and pan

Soft takeover: a fader does nothing until it reaches the value it controls; the screen shows which way to move it (F2^ up, F2v down). Turn it off with soft_takeover (Script settings).

Pad bank B

Pad bank B is in one of eight pad modes (Pad modes): steps, channels, patterns, mute, solo, arm, performance, route. Oct- + pads 9-16 pick one; hold the Shift pad to see them lit. Pad bank A always plays notes into the selected channel.

ControlDoes
Oct- + pad 1 / 2Mixer inserts −8 / +8
Oct- + pad 3 / 4Previous / next pattern
Oct- + pad 5Capture: the notes you just played (the last minute) go into the pattern
Oct- + pad 6 / 7Undo / Redo
Oct- + pad 8A new empty pattern
Shift pad + Left / RightThe pads' page

Pad names. Hold Oct- (with Settings → Display → Hints on) and the keyboard lists all 16 pads of bank B with what a plain tap does right now: the step number in Steps, channel, pattern or insert names in their colours, Mute, Solo, Arm or Route on the bottom row, the transport names in the Overview. Needs firmware 0.90.8.

The Overview's bottom row is the transport instead of the pad mode's own row (Overview); Oct- + a bottom pad still picks the pad mode.

The page line

In the Device view the screen's page line shows the plugin's parameter banks by the numbers of their parameters: 1-8, 9-16, and so on, the last one ending at the parameter count (for example 17-21). It is empty in the other views, when there is no plugin, and while a step is held.

FL's windows

The keyboard follows FL. Select an insert in FL (with the mouse or a pad) and the Mixer view shows the bank of 8 holding it and the fader half it sits in. Open FL's mixer window and the keyboard switches to the Mixer view; click the channel rack, the playlist, the piano roll or a plugin window and it goes back to the Device view. Turn the window following off with follow_window (Script settings).

Knobs

Turning a knob changes the parameter at once, 128 steps over its range. Hold Oct- or the Shift pad while turning for fine steps (1024). In every view, a held step (Steps mode) borrows K1-K8 for its own settings.
