What the screen shows

Tempo, the play position as bar.beat.sixteenth, the time signature (FL reports beats per bar), and the switches: metronome, loop recording, precount. The status bar says Song or Pattern, and the position line names the pattern in pattern mode.

The transport row

In this view the bottom row of pad bank B (pads 9-16) is the transport. A tap runs the function; the switches are bright while on and dim otherwise.

PadFunctionColour
9Stopwhite
10Playgreen
11Recred
12Song (song / pattern mode)amber
13Loop recamber
14Metro (metronome)cyan
15Marker < (previous marker)magenta
16Marker > (next marker)magenta

The top row stays the current pad mode's top row (in Steps, steps 1-8 of the page). Oct- or the Shift pad + a bottom pad still picks the pad mode. In Steps, steps 9-16 of the page are not on the pads in this view; in the Mixer and Device views the bottom row is the mode's own again. Hold Oct- (Hints on) to see the names.

The functions are the list pad_map in oscfl_settings.py (Script settings). Also available: Countdown, Step edit, Marker + (a marker at the playhead), Undo, Redo, Capture, Tap. The markers use FL's arrangement functions and need a recent FL Studio.
