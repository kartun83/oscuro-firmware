Program a beat

  1. Select the drum channel: Channels mode (Oct- + pad 10), tap its pad on the top row.
  2. Steps mode (Oct- + pad 9): tap the steps you want.
  3. Hold a step and turn K1 for an accent (velocity) or K7 for a little swing (shift).
  4. Back to Channels for the next sound; play fills on pad bank A.

Write and arrange patterns

  1. Patterns mode (Oct- + pad 11): tap an empty pad for a new pattern.
  2. Play a part on the keys, then Oct- + pad 5 (Capture) to put what you played into the pattern, or record with SEQ REC.
  3. Oct- + SEQ REC switches to song mode to hear the playlist.

Shape a sound

Select the channel, stay in the Device view, turn K1-K8, page with Left / Right. Open an effect window with the mouse and the knobs follow it. Oct- + Left / Right walks the instrument and its insert's effects.

Mix and route

SEQ to the Mixer view: faders set four volumes, K1-K8 pan. In the Device view K11 sends the selected channel to another insert. Route mode (Oct- + pad 16) builds sends between inserts.

Perform live

Set up performance mode in the playlist, then Perform mode (Oct- + pad 15): launch clips from the pads, hold to stop a track, Shift pad + Right for the next blocks.
