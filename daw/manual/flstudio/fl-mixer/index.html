What the screen shows

Eight strips of FL's mixer inserts, from insert 1. The selected insert has a white frame. Squares under each strip: mute yellow, solo blue, arm red. The meters move while FL plays. FL's mixer has no group tracks, so no group lines appear.

Controls

ControlDoes
K1-K8Pan of the 8 inserts
K9-K16 (Knob Bank B)Stereo separation of the 8 inserts
Faders 1-4Volumes of the 4 inserts in the fader window (marked F1-F4)
Right / LeftMove the fader window to strips 5-8 / 1-4; past the end, the next / previous 8 inserts
Oct- + Left / RightPrevious / next 8 inserts

It follows FL

Select an insert in FL, with the mouse, a pad or anything else, and the screen moves to the bank of 8 holding it; the fader window becomes the half (strips 1-4 or 5-8) the insert sits in, and those faders need to be picked up again. Only a change of the selected insert moves anything: if you page with Left / Right or Oct- + Left / Right the screen stays where you put it until you select another insert. The master and an insert beyond the last one do not move it.

Opening FL's mixer window also opens this view, and clicking another FL window (channel rack, playlist, piano roll, plugin) goes back to the Device view (follow_window, Script settings). SEQ still cycles the views.

Mute, solo, arm and routing from the pads

The pad modes Mute, Solo, Arm and Route (Pad modes) put the same eight inserts on pad bank B: the top row selects an insert, the bottom row mutes, solos or arms it, or, in Route, sends the selected insert to it.
