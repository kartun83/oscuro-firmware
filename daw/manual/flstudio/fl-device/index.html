Which plugin

The knobs follow what you work on in FL:

  1. an effect window you opened in the mixer (the focused effect), else
  2. the instrument of the selected channel in the channel rack.

Oct- + Left / Right steps through the selected channel's instrument and the effects in the slots of its mixer insert; the screen says Plugin: <name>. Selecting another channel, or focusing another window, follows FL again.

The status bar shows the bank, for example 2/5, plus Fx3 when the plugin sits in mixer slot 3, and the pad mode on the right.

K1-K8: the plugin

ControlDoes
K1-K8Eight named parameters of the plugin
Left / RightPrevious / next eight
Oct- + Left / RightPrevious / next plugin

Only parameters with a name are shown: a VST's empty slots and MIDI CC entries are skipped. Value text comes from the plugin where it gives one (4.82 kHz); otherwise the screen shows a percentage. A channel with no plugin (an audio clip) shows no plugin and dim knobs.

K9-K16: the channel and its insert (Knob Bank B)

KnobDoes
K9Channel volume
K10Channel pan
K11Insert: the mixer insert the channel plays into, one insert a detent (routing from the keyboard)
K12That insert's volume
K13That insert's pan
K14That insert's stereo separation
K15Master volume
K16—

What FL does not have

FL does not mark modulated parameters, so the knobs show no modulation tick; and it has no "touch" for parameters, so whether FL records a knob turn as automation depends on FL's own recording settings.
