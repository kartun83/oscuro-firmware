9 Pad modes: steps, channels, patterns, performance, routing

Pad bank B is where FL Studio shines on this keyboard: its step sequencer, the channel rack, patterns, performance mode and the mixer's routing are each one chord away. Pad bank A keeps playing notes into the selected channel.

Picking a mode

Hold Oct- and tap a bottom-row pad (9-16); hold the Shift pad to see the eight modes lit, the current one bright. The mode stays when you change views. The screen names the new mode, for example Pads: Steps of Kick.

Shift pad + Left / Right turns the page: steps by 16, channels by 8, patterns by 16, performance blocks by one.

Pad names. Hold Oct- with Settings → Display → Hints on (firmware 0.90.8) and the keyboard names every pad by what a plain tap does now: in Steps the step number (blank beyond the pattern's length), in Channels, Mute, Solo, Arm and Route the channel or insert names in their colours with Mute, Solo, Arm or Route under them, in Patterns the pattern names (an empty slot reads New), in Perform the playlist track names (blank while performance mode is off). With Hints off nothing shows and the pads work as always.

In the Overview the bottom row is the transport (Stop, Play, Rec, Song, Loop rec, Metro, Marker <, Marker >) whatever the mode; the top row stays the mode's top row, and Oct- + a bottom pad still picks the mode (Overview).

Steps

The sixteen pads are sixteen steps of the selected channel in the current pattern, FL's step sequencer: lit in the channel's colour where a step is set, dim where it is empty (each beat a little brighter), white for the step playing now. The status bar shows Steps 1-16/16.

Pick another channel (Channels mode, the Overview's K7, or the mouse) and the pads show its steps. Longer patterns have more pages.

Channels

The channel rack is your drum kit: the top row selects eight channels (lit in their colours, the selected one bright), the bottom row mutes them. Select a channel here, then switch to Steps to program it, or play it on pad bank A.

Patterns

Sixteen patterns: the current one bright, patterns with content dim, empty ones nearly dark. A tap selects that pattern; an empty pad makes a new pattern there.

Mute, Solo, Arm

The top row selects one of eight mixer inserts (in their colours), the bottom row toggles its mute, solo or record arm. Oct- + pads 1 / 2 move to the previous / next eight inserts.

Perform

FL's performance mode on the pads: eight playlist tracks across, the current block on the top row and the next block on the bottom row. Turn performance mode on in the playlist first; until then the pads stay dark and the screen says so.

Route

The bottom row shows where the selected insert sends: lit where it routes to that insert. A tap adds or removes the route, as FL's mixer does when you click a route switch. The selected insert's own pad stays dark.
