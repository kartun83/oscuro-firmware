03 · One language

Tailored. Never fragmented.

Each build is tuned for a different priority, and all of them speak the same language. What you learn on one carries to the next.

One grammar

Oct- is the shift key. Left and Right go in and out. Dots show where you are in a carousel. Learn it once and it works on every screen of every build.

One colour language

A flavour has one colour. You see it on the pads, on the screen header and on its row on this page.

One standard of proof

Every claim carries a badge: measured on the unit, in a test build, or planned. Nothing is presented as finished before it sounds.

The same four gestures, on four different screens

04 · Viroid

One thing, done properly: synthesis.

Viroid turns your SMK-37 into a true instrument. Virus-inspired oscillators, filters and modulation, heavily optimised for this hardware, loading original Virus patches. It does not try to be a groovebox, a sampler and a looper too.

  • A Virus-inspired voiceTest buildTwo oscillators with sync and FM, a sub oscillator, noise, a ring modulator, two filters in the Virus's four routings, saturation, two envelopes and three LFOs.
  • HyperSawTest buildEither oscillator can become a stack of up to nine detuned saws, with density and spread on two knobs. A setting caps the total so you decide how much of the CPU it may take.
  • Original Virus patchesTest buildSend Access Virus A, B, C and TI patches from your computer and they play, including the delay and reverb stored in them and the TI's LFO shapes.
  • Measured, not guessedTest buildFilter, envelope and LFO curves follow measurements of a Virus TI, so a patch behaves the way its maker intended.

Synthesis is all it does. It does that on every cycle the hardware has.

05 · Other synths

The legends in one build, and drums on their own.

Retrowave puts the classic engines side by side and lets you switch between them from the keys. Tympanika and Phantasm are for drum-first music.

  • Five engines, one buildTest buildCZ-1, DX7 FM, Juno-60, Moog and MT drums. Hold Oct- and press PATCH to switch; each engine keeps its own patches and banks.
  • DX7 FMTest buildThe DX7 core of Dexed, 12 notes at once. Load DX7 cartridges and single voices from the installer page.
  • Casio CZTest buildPhase-distortion voices with eight waveforms, and 8, 6 or 4 notes depending on the mode.
  • Juno-60 and MoogTest buildSix Juno voices and four Moog voices, each with its own filter knobs, chorus and patches.

06 · DAW

A controller with ideas.

The DAW build puts the pads, keys and knobs at the service of your session: it suggests chords, names what you play, shares tempo and scale with your DAW and sends clock to other gear.

  • Harmony HelperTest buildThe pads show the seven best chords to play next, coloured by how well they fit, with the chords of your scale underneath.
  • 39 genres, your moodTest buildPick a genre and a mood and choose how bold the suggestions get: Safe, Tonal or Bold.
  • It names what you playTest buildChords played on the keys are named, even odd ones like 7#9, 6/9 or slash chords, and it can notice when you move to another scale.
  • Scale and ChordTest buildLock the keys to a scale and play full chords from single keys; a DAW can set the scale too.

07 · Arpeggiator

From simple up-and-down to patterns that evolve.

Five sections, all optional. With them off, the arp plays exactly as a classic arp does; switch them on one at a time and it starts to breathe.

  • Twenty directionsTest buildUp, Down, Inclusive and Exclusive, starting upward or downward, Thumb and Pinky, Converge, Diverge, Chord, Order, User and three kinds of random.
  • Rates, gate and swingTest buildThirteen rates from half notes to triplet thirty-seconds, one to four octaves, tempo from 30 to 300 BPM, gate and swing, and Latch.
  • RhythmTest buildEuclidean patterns: choose hits, length and rotation, add a chance for each hit and an accent on the first.
  • EvolveTest buildThe phrase drifts and comes back. Set how often it changes, how much, and how far it reaches; freeze it, thaw it or reseed it with one key.

08 · Sequencer

Built to perform with.

Two melodic parts and a drum part, each with eight patterns. Three screens, and the one you perform on keeps your hands on the pads.

  • Three partsTest buildSeq1 and Seq2 for melody, and a drum part with 14 lanes. Each part has 8 patterns of up to 64 steps.
  • HOME: performTest buildPads mute and unmute lanes and parts, white keys pick patterns, and changes can wait for the bar or the end of the pattern.
  • EDIT: stepsTest buildPads are steps. Hold one and the knobs edit that step: velocity, gate, probability, ratchet, micro-timing, condition and a filter lock.
  • Record how you likeTest buildLive or step by step, overdub or overwrite, quantise on or off, with a one or two bar count-in.

09 · Make it yours

Everyone plays differently.

So the feel is a setting, not a decision made for you. Sensitivity, scan speed, curves, which knob does what, even the colours are yours to set.

  • Pad responseTest buildThreshold, sensitivity and wait time, then a velocity curve: from light touch to hard hits.
  • Scan speedTest buildChoose how often the controls are read, from 8 to 24 kHz in half-kilohertz steps, or leave it on Auto. A lower setting asks the processor to read the controls less often.
  • Voice limitTest buildSet the most notes that may sound at once to match what your patches need.
  • Knob and velocity curvesTest buildShape how knobs respond and how key and pad velocity map to loudness.

11 · Safe by design

Easy to try. Easy to undo.

Putting Oscuro on your keyboard changes one area of its memory, and every step can be reversed.

Only the app is written

The keyboard's boot code, your settings and its calibration stay exactly as they are.

Back to M-Vave in minutes

The installer puts M-Vave's own firmware back at any time, from Oscuro or from any M-Vave version.

Your data is not touched

Oscuro never erases M-Vave's user data by itself. Save a backup of the patch memory first and restore it later.

A safe start

Hold Globe while switching on to skip Bluetooth, the faster clock and other optional extras.

12 · Install

A few minutes in a browser.

Three steps, nothing to install, and every update can be undone.

  1. 1Close your DAW, connect the keyboard and open the installer page.
  2. 2Choose the Oscuro file for the build you want. For a first install also choose M-Vave's own firmware file.
  3. 3Press Install and keep the cable connected until it says Finished.
