Install

Discover the dark side of your SMK-37.

Our own installer, online or on your computer. Flashing firmware always carries risk.

Download Oscuro Viroid

The download goes live with the first public build. This page is ready for it.

Two ways to install

Online

Install from your browser

Open the installer in Chrome or Edge, connect the keyboard and follow the prompts. Nothing to download first.

It depends on your connection. The firmware is fetched while it is written, so a dropped or slow connection, a sleeping computer or a pulled cable can interrupt the write, and an interrupted write can leave the keyboard unable to start. Use a wired connection and keep the keyboard plugged in until it restarts.

Open the installer

Available with the first public build.

From a file

Install from your computer

Download the installer and the firmware once, then run the installer from your computer. Nothing is fetched while it writes.

The steadier choice. Everything is on your computer before anything is written, so the connection can come and go and the write does not care.

Download the local installer

Available with the first public build.

The keyboard restarts into Oscuro and starts on the synth screen.

What you need

  • Your M-Vave SMK-37 Elite or a compatible model (SMK-37 Pro, Donner Starrykey-37PLAY, EROK MKE-P37) and its USB cable.
  • A computer with Chrome or Edge to run the installer, online or local.
  • A steady connection and a computer that will not sleep while it writes.

What it leaves out

Oscuro Viroid has no Bluetooth and no step sequencer. That memory goes to the synth and the effects. Oscuro DAW, in development, is the build for those.

On your computer the keyboard appears as “SMK-37 Oscuro USB”. A DAW or script that remembers M-Vave's device name may need the keyboard selected again.

Flash with care.

Your risk

If a write goes wrong, the keyboard may stop starting. Install only on hardware you own, and only if you accept that.

Our own installer

Oscuro comes with an installer of its own, which you can run online or from a local copy.

Keep it steady

Use a good cable, keep the keyboard plugged in and the computer awake until it has restarted.

A gentle start

Hold Globe while switching on to skip optional extras such as the faster clock.
