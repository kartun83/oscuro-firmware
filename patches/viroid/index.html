One file per synth, written for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any other AI chat. No tools, no Python: copy it, say what you want, load what you get back.

What the AI can't do

The guide tells the AI to say these things at the end of its answer. Play the bank, tell the AI what to change, and load the new one.

End every answer that contains a bank with a short paragraph headed What I couldn't do, in your own words. It covers:

  • You cannot hear the sounds. The values are informed estimates from how the engine works, not tuned by ear; some patches will need adjusting on the keyboard.
  • Anything the player asked for that this engine cannot do, and what you made instead.
  • Values you had to guess, or patches you are least sure of.
  • Whether you built the file yourself, or the player has to paste your text into the installer. If the packer reported an error you could not fix, say which patch and what the error said.

Never claim that you listened to, played or tested the sounds.

For Viroid in particular:

  • An FX rack stored with a patch plays only on the Viroid TI Rack build. On other Viroid builds the voice plays, and its delay and reverb follow the rack's first Delay and Reverb (the packer does that for you).
  • Wavetable, Wave PWM, Grain and Formant oscillators need the TI image's tables (the Viroid TI Rack build). Classic and HyperSaw play on every Viroid build.
  • No samples, no vocoder, no audio input, no comb or vowel filter bank, no ROM arpeggiator patterns.
  • There is a processing limit: HyperSaw saws times unison voices must stay at 11 or below (the packer refuses more). Prefer one HyperSaw oscillator with little unison over two dense ones.

Say so when a request needs them.

How it works (for the curious)

The tools and starter patches named here (tools/ and patches/) come with Oscuro's firmware packages. Each guide describes the firmware as of the version in the footer.

Viroid plays Access Virus patches. There is no JSON route here: the patch format is the Access Virus single dump. Besides the sounds you already have, Oscuro ships showcase banks for Viroid (patches/viroid_ti/ in the packages, their README.md lists every patch): Virus TI singles (.syx) and Oscuro banks with an FX rack per patch (.oscbank). The installer's Synth patches tab offers them in its Starter bank list. General matters (what a bank is, the patch memory) are in README.md next to this file.

Where Virus patches come from

  • Your own collection: single-patch dumps from Virus A, B, C and TI/TI2/Snow, as .syx or .mid files, one patch per file or a whole bank per file.
  • Any Virus editor or librarian that can save a bank or a single patch as SysEx.
  • Sound packs and soundsets you own.

Loading them

  1. Run the Viroid build (it is the only engine in that build).
  2. Open the installer page, Synth patches, choose the files and the Bank the patches go to (4 banks of 128), and send. A file with more patches than the bank holds continues into the next bank.
  3. Two optional boxes on that tab tidy a big collection before it is sent: Skip duplicate sounds (a copy under another name or category; the first one stays) and Skip placeholder patches (patches named only with dashes, underscores or INIT, which sound packs use as separators and credits).

The patch memory first needs the backup-and-erase step described in the user guide (Patch memory backup).

Editing on the keyboard

K1 to K8 edit the macros of the current patch (cutoff, resonance, envelope, oscillator shape, volume, ...); to save the changes, hold Oct - and press Right (Right again overwrites the stored patch, Left stores a copy). Each Virus patch also carries a modulation matrix of 6 slots, each with one source and up to three destinations: ../MODULATION_MATRIX.md explains how to edit it on the keyboard (hold Oct -, press Left).

Making new Virus patches

Make them in an editor or on a Virus and bring them over as above. The importer reads the published layout of the single dump (267 bytes for A/B/C, 524 for TI) and keeps the patch name, the category and the sound parameters.
