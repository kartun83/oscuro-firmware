Making your own MT drum kits

MT DRUMS is a Microtonic-style drum synth: eight channels, each with two stored sounds (L and R, 25 settings each) and a Morph that blends L into R. One patch is a whole kit (416 bytes). Notes 36 to 43 play the eight channels at the kit's morph position; notes 44 to 51 play the same channels pinned to side R. General matters (what a bank is, the patch memory, using an LLM) are in README.md next to this file. The factory kit banks are in patches/mt/: a jungle set imported from Microtonic kits and genre sets (house, electro, drum and bass, psy trance, ...).

In the builds: Retrowave, Phosphor, Tympanika

The tools and starter patches named here (tools/ and patches/) come with Oscuro's firmware packages. Each guide describes the firmware as of the version in the footer.

There are two ways to make kits:

  • Import Microtonic kits. tools/mt_import.py turns kits exported as snappysnap JSON (a list of {"index": n, "value": 0..1} pairs per kit) into a bank. Give it one kit file, or a folder of files named NN_name.json: python3 tools/mt_import.py kits_folder -o my_kits.oscbank --bank-name "My kits". Side R starts as a copy of side L, so the morph does nothing until you edit it.
  • Describe kits in numbers. llm-prompts/llm-bank-crafting-prompt.md is a complete specification written to be pasted into an LLM: the file format, the 25 parameters with the formulas between bytes and Hz, milliseconds and dB, recipes per genre, and a packer script that turns the LLM's answer into a valid bank. Read it yourself too: it is the best reference.

Then open the installer page, Synth patches, choose the .oscbank and send. A bank holds 16 kits. In the Retrowave and Phosphor builds the installer switches the keyboard to the engine of the file by itself; to switch by hand, hold Oct -, press PATCH, turn K1 to MT DRUMS and press >.

The kit

PartMeaning
8 channelsusual roles: 1 kick, 2 snare, 3 closed hat, 4 open hat, 5 perc or rim, 6 perc or tom, 7 clap or ride, 8 crash or FX
2 sidesL and R each hold all 25 parameters of every channel; the Morph (0 = all L, 255 = all R) blends them
nameup to 10 ASCII characters

The 25 parameters of a channel

Continuous values are 0 to 255; steps store the step index (the morph switches them from L to R at 128).
