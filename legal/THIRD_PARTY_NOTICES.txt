## Plaits / stmlib — the Mutable-family engine (`pl`)

`firmware/core/engine/pl/` (roadmap §21) is a C transcription of MIT-licensed
code by Emilie Gillet from two pinned upstreams:

- `pl_stmlib.[ch]` and `pl_pitch_tables.h` ← **stmlib** (https://github.com/pichenettes/stmlib, commit `e3bd7c9`): `Random`, `ParameterInterpolator`, `Svf`, `SemitonesToRatio`, polyblep, and the pitch LUTs.
- `pl_noise.[ch]` ← **eurorack/Plaits** (https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack, commit `08460a6`): `NoiseEngine` and `ClockedNoise`.

Full MIT text and per-file attribution are in [`core/engine/pl/NOTICE`](core/engine/pl/NOTICE).

**Changed in the port:** C++ → C; templates (`Svf::Process<mode>`,
`set_f_q<approx>`) flattened to named functions. The stmlib `Sqrt` is `sqrtf`
under `-DTEST` and the port keeps `sqrtf`; that is RT-safe because the xip
build links libm into the hot/SRAM group - the same reason the juno/moog
engines may call `expf`/`powf`/`tanf` - and `check_xip` confirms the real-time
graph never reaches flash. FMA contraction is disabled in the port
(`#pragma clang fp contract(off)` placed before every include, so it also covers
the inlined header helpers) and in the reference generator
(`tools/plaits_ref/gen.sh`, `-ffp-contract=off`) so the two round identically;
`tests/test_pl_noise.c` and `tests/test_pl_string.c` assert bit-for-bit equality
against the committed golden.

## cz-1 — the Casio CZ engine (`cz`)

`firmware/core/engine/engine_cz.c` and `firmware/core/engine/cz/cz_pd.[ch]`
(roadmap §24) are **keysmith's own** single-precision-float code. They
implement the *behaviour* of two publicly documented things; no third-party
source file is copied, translated or vendored, so no copyleft attaches to the
firmware:

- **The Casio CZ tone layout and playback behaviour** — the eight
  phase-distortion waveforms, three eight-step rate/level envelopes per line
  with explicit sustain and end points (the uPD933's separate DCO/DCW/DCA
  domains), the logarithmic DCA amplitude law, ring and noise modulation,
  vibrato, key-follow and per-envelope velocity sensitivity. These come from
  the **Casio CZ-1 System Exclusive specification** and owner's manual (public
  format/behaviour documents, not code) — the same tone the `cz-tone` patch
  format in `core/engine/ks_patch.c` reads and writes byte-for-byte. Facts and
  formats (byte offsets, parameter meaning) are not copyrightable expression.

- **Design references (credit only, no code copied):**
  - **Keith Adler's pdsynth** (https://github.com/keithadler/pdsynth,
    GPL-2.0-or-later) — consulted for the *architecture* of a phase-distortion
    instrument (the eight-waveform set, three envelopes per line, the
    bend-ceiling idea that stops high notes folding, the 2-to-4-line model,
    ring/noise mod) and read to confirm the Casio dump framing (nibble-encoded, low nibble first: a fact of the Casio format). None of its source is used; the DSP here is written from
    the Casio behaviour, and pdsynth's GPL-2.0 code is deliberately kept out of
    the tree (the reason cz-1 is a re-implementation, not a port).
  - **Devin Acker's MAME uPD933 device model** (the rate law `(8|(r&7))<<(r>>3)`, the level shifts, the transfer-function and window cases and the DCA table exponent are reimplemented from it as formulas, not copied)
    (https://github.com/mamedev/mame/blob/master/src/devices/sound/upd933.cpp,
    BSD-3-Clause) and Kerem Kilic's fixed-point adaptation in **melodee**
    (`cz_native.c`, BSD-3 DSP under a GPL-3 wrapper) — read to understand the
    chip's phase-function, envelope-rate and log-amplitude behaviour and the
    native 144-byte tone. **No code from either is copied**; melodee's GPL
    engine wrapper (`eng_cz.c`) is not used, and its fixed-point internals were
    re-derived as float. This is the documented-behaviour basis, explicitly
    *not* bit-exact to a real CZ-1.

If a future milestone vendors an actual third-party CZ DSP file, the applicable
licence text and a §4(b)-style change notice must be added here first.


## qf_math — bounded Viroid control exponential

`core/engine/va/va_dsp.c` cherry-picks the degree-five polynomial and
round-to-nearest exponent split from `qf_pow2` in [qf_math](https://github.com/deftio/qf_math),
commit `293bf6806d2c74acf6198ad05d6d7e00f232e79c` (BSD-2-Clause).
Adapted for Oscuro: restricted to [-24,24], libm fallback outside that domain,
and direct exponent adjustment within its guaranteed normal-result range.
The polynomial is algebraically translated from `P(f)` on [-0.5,0.5] to
`sqrt(2)*P(f-0.5)` on [0,1], then its constant and highest coefficient are
normalized to give endpoints 1 and 2. A floor split replaces the upstream
mantissa-offset split. All coefficients are positive, so rounded Horner
operations remain monotone; this avoids upstream's tiny negative-residual
and half-integer boundary reversals.
Only modulated filter cutoff and polyphonic LFO key follow use it.
