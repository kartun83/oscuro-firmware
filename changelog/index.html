Changelog

What changed, newest first.

Written for players. Every version adds a line for what you can notice on the keyboard.

The changelog is the project's own record and is kept in English.

0.80

17 releases

0.80.16 2026-10-21

  • Fixed reject weak pad noise that changed pages, stop voice-cap collapse under fixed FX load, and clarify physical scan controls.
  • Changed brighter dim page pads and more distinct neighboring FX colors.

0.80.15 2026-10-20

  • Fixed distinguish effect page colors and PARA/FX lights; add a native FX bypass shortcut and pad-navigation diagnostics.
  • Added choose a scan ceiling in 500-line/s steps, with Auto preserving the existing response.

0.80.14 2026-10-19

  • Added semantic page-pad colors and full-rack page navigation; bounded filter/LFO control math and a separate 128-frame rack experiment.
  • Fixed rapid FX rack page changes preserve type defaults and subsequent knob edits.

0.80.13 2026-10-19

  • Fixed restored the live bottom bar, simplified PARA pages and lit page-selection pads; steadier button-bank refresh.
  • Added separate private Viroid full FX rack test build with topology controls.

0.80.12 2026-10-19

  • Added Viroid Delay/Reverb pages, carousel indicators and pad shortcuts for parameter and effects pages.
  • Fixed preserve queued parameter edits when changing pages.

0.80.11 2026-10-19

  • Fixed service audio during screen pixel and font work and avoid unnecessary processing of exact silence.

0.80.10 2026-10-19

  • Fixed speed up screen transfers while preserving audio and control servicing.

0.80.9 2026-10-19

  • Fixed let pad inputs settle before sampling to prevent a hard press triggering another pad.

0.80.8 2026-10-19

  • Changed overlap key and encoder decoding with analog conversion to reduce control scanning waits.

0.80.7 2026-10-19

  • Changed test reports separate analog acquisition and scan processing costs to guide further responsiveness improvements.

0.80.6 2026-10-19

  • Fixed removed an artificial diagnostic wait that could interrupt audio and controls while reading test reports.

0.80.5 2026-10-19

  • Fixed avoid unused analog conversions while preserving control sampling.

0.80.4 2026-10-19

  • Fixed keep audio and controls serviced while reading diagnostic stack usage.

0.80.3 2026-10-19

  • Fixed keep audio and controls serviced between screen layout objects.

0.80.2 2026-10-18

  • Fixed overlap matrix transfer with analog sampling to reduce control scanning waits.
  • Changed test reports separate recent display stalls from startup timing.

0.80.1 2026-10-18

  • Fixed pending audio takes priority at screen service points instead of waiting for the scan timer or an input scan.
  • Fixed diagnostic CPU reports separate poll work from service yields and explain that independent peaks cannot be added.

0.80.0 2026-10-18

  • Added Viroid TI identifies itself on the splash screen, in the installer and in diagnostic reports.
  • Fixed adaptive Viroid polyphony leaves time for controls and screen updates instead of budgeting audio alone.

0.71

2 releases

0.71.1 2026-10-17

  • Fixed Viroid's filter was far too dark and too gentle: the cutoff now follows the Virus (open filters really open), resonance has the Virus peak and level, and filter 1 key follow is signed (64 = off).
  • Changed Viroid envelopes and LFOs follow the Virus: attacks are linear ramps, decays and releases run several times longer in the middle of the range, amplitude sustain is convex, and LFO rates are slower from the middle up.
  • Fixed the sub oscillator level now follows the Virus (a triangle sub was far too quiet, a square too loud)
  • Fixed oscillator 2 FM amount now sweeps as widely as the Virus (the top of the range was far too mild)
  • Changed oscillator 2 fine detune follows the Virus (in tune near the middle, flat below it, narrower on high notes), so detune 0 is now slightly flat and imported patches line up
