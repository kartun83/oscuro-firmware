Changelog

What changed in each release.

Only marked releases, each one covering every test update since the one before. New things first; fixes in their own section.

The changelog is the project's own record and is kept in English.

0.80

2026-10-21 · covers 0.73.0 to 0.80.16 · 25 test updates

New 9

  • choose a scan ceiling in 500-line/s steps, with Auto preserving the existing response.
  • semantic page-pad colors and full-rack page navigation; bounded filter/LFO control math and a separate 128-frame rack experiment.
  • separate private Viroid full FX rack test build with topology controls.
  • Viroid Delay/Reverb pages, carousel indicators and pad shortcuts for parameter and effects pages.
  • Viroid TI identifies itself on the splash screen, in the installer and in diagnostic reports.
  • Harmony Helper carousel, anchor/PIN/DEL/STORE pads, progression memory, velocity choice, Oct- scale shift, bass assist
  • Harmony Helper (DAW build): next-chord pads with genre and mood, chord and scale detection
  • Viroid plays the delay and reverb stored in Virus patches (send, time, feedback, color, decay, damping, pre-delay); the Viroid-TI test image keeps 2 sequencer patterns per track to make room.
  • Viroid-TI plays the Virus TI's LFO waves 6-67 (pick them as the LFO shape); the TI wave bank is stored smaller.

Improved 7

  • brighter dim page pads and more distinct neighboring FX colors.
  • overlap key and encoder decoding with analog conversion to reduce control scanning waits.
  • test reports separate analog acquisition and scan processing costs to guide further responsiveness improvements.
  • test reports separate recent display stalls from startup timing.
  • Viroid adapts its voice ceiling to audio headroom and avoids idle voice work; controls give audio more frequent service.
  • the Viroid-TI test image keeps 4 sequencer patterns per track and a 256-note capture history to make room for delay and reverb at full size.
  • Viroid's Max Voices setting now limits the polyphony (it did nothing before); test builds run up to 32 voices so the unit's ceiling can be measured.

Fixed 17

  • reject weak pad noise that changed pages, stop voice-cap collapse under fixed FX load, and clarify physical scan controls.
  • distinguish effect page colors and PARA/FX lights; add a native FX bypass shortcut and pad-navigation diagnostics.
  • rapid FX rack page changes preserve type defaults and subsequent knob edits.
  • restored the live bottom bar, simplified PARA pages and lit page-selection pads; steadier button-bank refresh.
  • preserve queued parameter edits when changing pages.
  • service audio during screen pixel and font work and avoid unnecessary processing of exact silence.
  • speed up screen transfers while preserving audio and control servicing.
  • let pad inputs settle before sampling to prevent a hard press triggering another pad.
  • removed an artificial diagnostic wait that could interrupt audio and controls while reading test reports.
  • avoid unused analog conversions while preserving control sampling.
  • keep audio and controls serviced while reading diagnostic stack usage.
  • keep audio and controls serviced between screen layout objects.
  • overlap matrix transfer with analog sampling to reduce control scanning waits.
  • pending audio takes priority at screen service points instead of waiting for the scan timer or an input scan.
  • diagnostic CPU reports separate poll work from service yields and explain that independent peaks cannot be added.
  • adaptive Viroid polyphony leaves time for controls and screen updates instead of budgeting audio alone.
  • Viroid no longer cuts voices for screen or USB delays; patch transfers and screen updates service audio more often.

0.72

2026-10-17 · covers 0.69.0 to 0.72.0 · 13 test updates

New 6

  • Tympanika and Phantasm About screens (Drift)
  • assignable controls: Settings > Physical Controls > Bank B Knobs / Bank B Faders assign K1-K8 and F1-F4 on bank B to a Synth, FX rack or Mixer parameter; faders pick up and the fader popup marks the value they must reach; assignments are saved and follow a flash project (DX7 build)
  • Settings > Display > Hints (On by default): hold Oct- (on the synth home, with a fader, or in the sequencer) or SEQ (in the sequencer) for a quarter of a second and a card shows what the next key does; it never blocks the combination
  • live recording options in SEQ MORE > Record: Overdub or Overwrite (erases the lane you record on for one pass), Quantize On/Off (Off keeps the exact timing) and a 1 or 2 bar Count-in. Quantize is On by default, so hits now sit on their step; turn it Off for the previous behaviour
  • Settings > Display > Stats shows CPU load, free RAM and sounding voices in the header (off by default)
  • Retrowave pads 9-16 recall patches, separately for each engine and remembered over power-off: tap to load, hold an empty pad to store the current patch, hold and turn K1 to replace, hold and press Left to clear

Improved 9

  • the Analog 1-4 pole filters now follow the Virus TI's Analog filter (level, resonance, drive), so resonant patches sound different.
  • oscillator volume, noise level and Saturation curves 1-6 follow the Virus TI; mid-range oscillator volumes are quieter and the noise no longer follows the oscillator volume.
  • Viroid envelopes and LFOs follow the Virus: attacks are linear ramps, decays and releases run several times longer in the middle of the range, amplitude sustain is convex, and LFO rates are slower from the middle up.
  • oscillator 2 fine detune follows the Virus (in tune near the middle, flat below it, narrower on high notes), so detune 0 is now slightly flat and imported patches line up
  • the merged Retrowave build and the Bluetooth builds keep 512 captured events instead of 1024 to stay inside the unit's RAM; assignable bank B controls are not in the Retrowave build (every other build has them). Fixed: every build failed to link because the stats header's free-RAM readout had no limit symbol in the flash layout
  • the settings lists name the list you came from in the middle of the top bar (for example Physical Controls above Keys, Pads and Knobs)
  • settings regrouped: Physical Controls is now a list (Keys, Pads, Knobs, Wheels, Faders); Calibration became Wheels and Fader Setup became Faders, both moved under it; Synthesizer holds Max Voices. No setting was removed
  • settings pages are now lists: K1 moves the highlighted row, K2 changes it, K3 steps to the next or previous settings page (the dots at the bottom show where you are). Hold + knob quick edits are unchanged
  • internal clean-up of Left/Right page navigation (no change in behaviour)

Fixed 10

  • Viroid's filter was far too dark and too gentle: the cutoff now follows the Virus (open filters really open), resonance has the Virus peak and level, and filter 1 key follow is signed (64 = off).
  • the sub oscillator level now follows the Virus (a triangle sub was far too quiet, a square too loud)
  • oscillator 2 FM amount now sweeps as widely as the Virus (the top of the range was far too mild)
  • resting pitch and mod wheels no longer spam values after use (continuous centre dead zone, no flip at the end snap points)
  • faders no longer pop up their panel from electrical noise while untouched
  • a single pad touch no longer plays a second ghost note (rebound after a hard hit is ignored)
  • a Globe tap goes straight to Settings without flashing Play Setup first
  • Juno init patch was silent while a key was held (filter envelope polarity) and Cutoff above about 120 killed the voice
  • sequencer step rows repaint alone when only the playhead moves, so the screen keeps up while playing
  • the Bluetooth test note could stay stuck on and be resent when a packet was refused

0.68

2026-10-11 · covers 0.53.0 to 0.68.0 · 20 test updates

New 17

  • Bluetooth variants (_bt) of the Viroid and DAW builds (Retrowave and Viroid TI have no room for the radio); the DAW build is now a flash app like the others
  • Install-tab option "Don't check the keyboard's model" (off by default) for boards under other names; fixed the installer's final check on a Starrykey 37 Play with the 015 file
  • the installer accepts M-Vave's MKE-P37 and Starrykey 37 Play keyboards and their firmware files (same board as the SMK-37 Pro; not yet tried on a unit)
  • mt drum engine (Microtonic-style, 8 channels with L/R morph) selectable in the retrowave build, with the Microtonic jungle kit import in the installer; test build only
  • patches can be filtered by category on K3 in the Viroid patch list, with up to two category strips beside each patch and the category in the header
  • save a patch with Oct- + Right (Oct- alone steps the octave on release, as in the other views)
  • Sequencer beat lines (follow the time signature; MORE: Beat lines, Time sig)
  • Sequencer MORE screen (part settings, tools, project) and a SEQ-button cycle HOME - EDIT - MORE; Left/Right choose the part
  • Sequencer EDIT screen: lane matrix or piano roll, knob strip that switches between lane and held-step parameters, Oct pages through 64 steps
  • Sequencer HOME screen: lane and part mutes on the pads (change at the next pass), patterns on the white keys, compact or expanded drum view
  • Arp: live strum for chords played with the arp off (Strum section, Live strum and Window)
  • Arp: Evolve (Mutate, Every, Bars, Gravity, Reach) - the pattern drifts and returns
  • Perform layer: hold Oct - and tap pads to toggle arp features (Settings, Keys & Pads, Perform layer)
  • Arp: Evolve section with Phrase and Seed; Freeze, Thaw and Reseed (ARP + A# / A)
  • Arp: Rhythm section (Euclid hits, length, rotate, chance, accent)
  • Arp: Strum and Human sections (swipe Right on the ARP page), switch per section
  • Arp: 10 new directions (Thumb, Pinky, Dn Incl/Excl, Con+Div, Rand No Dup/Drift/Once) and rates 1/2, 1/2T, dotted 1/4, 1/8, 1/16

Improved 5

  • a package now holds Retrowave, Viroid, Viroid TI and DAW builds, plus Bluetooth variants of Viroid and DAW; DX7, Juno, Moog and Mutable builds only on request
  • the USB mouse mode and its Settings row are off in every build (the code stays in the source)
  • the user guide covers the Retrowave engines, the new sequencer screens, Bluetooth and the current Settings and limits, the tester guide lists what to try, and CHANGELOG.html lists the changes by topic
  • more non-audio code is compiled for size in the TI and Bluetooth builds, about 9 KB freed, sound unchanged
  • The private TI wavetable test build keeps the faster reader and uses 44 KiB table caches to fit the latest sequencer.

Fixed 5

  • the retrowave build with the mt drum engine now fits in memory; patch categories (strips, K3 filter) exist only in the Viroid build, and the UI, patch and sequencer-editor code compile for size there
  • Virus patches now show the unsaved star when a macro is edited, and long patch names no longer hide it
  • Viroid wavetable playback starts on the next phase sample, matching the TI oscillator.
  • the CZ-1 synth in the retrowave build releases notes, plays the right pitch and octave, and follows the Casio waveform, envelope and SysEx tone rules
  • the Bluetooth test build links again (FM lookup tables read from flash in that build only)

0.52

2026-10-09 · covers 0.47.0 to 0.52.4 · 10 test updates

New 6

  • FX rack Reverb (room, hall, plate); the rack's pool is 12 KB so the retrowave build keeps about 5 KB of SRAM spare, and DX7/Viroid/Mutable/Bluetooth builds leave the rack out
  • FX rack effects Tremolo/Auto-pan (tempo sync), Distortion (soft, hard, fold, tube), Bit-crush and Compressor
  • FX rack topologies: up to six effect slots wired as 2 Sends, Serial 2/3/6, 3 + 3, 2 + 2 + 2, Fan 3, Split-Merge and three Insert layouts (pick on the FX ROUTE page); an Off slot is a bypass
  • FX rack effects Phaser, Wah (auto/manual/LFO), Notch/Filter (notch, bell, LP, HP, BP with sweep) and 3-band EQ
  • FX rack (hold FX, press Right): two effect slots on the mixer's send buses with Route Send/Insert, Return and per-strip sends; first effects Delay (tempo sync, ping-pong), Chorus, Flanger
  • Knob Speed (Settings → Physical Controls): knobs on level-type parameters (synth, mixer, gate) move 2x per click by default, or 1x/4x/8x; lists and numbers like MIDI Program keep one step per click

Improved 2

  • the installer says why a patch could not be stored (memory full, patch memory not ready, or a value out of range)
  • Reverb has early reflections and a denser, wider onset

Fixed 2

  • DX7 banks with out-of-range values (such as the factory ROM banks) upload instead of failing; the values are clamped
  • Arc knob style shows a long two-word value on two lines instead of cutting it off

0.46

2026-10-09 · covers 0.36.0 to 0.46.1 · 19 test updates

New 8

  • cz-1, a Casio CZ phase-distortion synth (eight bent waveforms, per-line eight-step envelopes, ring and noise modulation) — it boots in the merged build, now called Retrowave, and FM, Juno and Moog still switch at runtime; a Mode knob picks CZ-only, Retrowave or Full; load and save real Casio CZ voice dumps
  • Settings > Display > Low Battery (Off, Warn, Protect): on battery, a warning near the empty level and, with Protect, the pad LEDs dim and then go dark so a nearly empty cell is not loaded into a reset.
  • USB Audio In (Settings > DAW & USB, next start): the computer's stereo 48 kHz sound plays through the unit's mixer (USB-in strip); not in Bluetooth builds. Test builds can start the audio link late (--pop-late) to trace the power-on pop.
  • fader bank 1 (F1-F4) is the mixer (master volume, balance, FX returns; Synth or USB-in strip on request) with pickup, Oct- held + a fader reaches the other bank, and a Mixer page in Settings. Bank 1 no longer sends CCs unless Mixer > Send CC is on.
  • a mixer behind the output (master volume and balance are stored for the faders to come); the metronome click is now a sound of its own, so it plays even with the synth off and never goes to USB audio
  • USB audio. Turn on Globe → DAW & USB → USB Audio and restart: the keyboard also shows up as a sound card, and the computer can record the synth (stereo, 48 kHz) over the USB cable. USB Gain sets the recording level (−12, −6, 0 or +6 dB; 0 dB is what the headphone jack gets). Off by default
  • the Mutable synth's Low/Mid/Hi TM modes now play a morphing wavetable engine (Plaits WavetableEngine, bit-exact)
  • the Mutable synth's Bass mode is now a real virtual-analog engine (Plaits VirtualAnalogEngine, bit-exact)

Improved 9

  • Viroid wavetable playback reads its tables up to 2.4 times faster, leaving more CPU headroom; the sound is bit-for-bit unchanged.
  • Viroid wavetable playback preserves TI interpolation and morph rounding in local table builds.
  • Viroid wavetable oscillators now build their waves and pick their band-limit level with the exact method recovered from the Virus TI, so wavetable patches should sound closer to the original at high and low notes.
  • the output mixer and metronome click skip work they do not need, and unchanged pad lights are not resent every frame; the sound is bit for bit the same.
  • DX7 build, sequencer Projects page: saving and loading run outside the sequencer's timing and show the result; loading is refused while the sequencer plays. Projects do not fit the flash reserved for them yet, so for now it answers "Project not saved/loaded"
  • the all-in-one build now holds DX7 FM, Juno-60 and Moog; Viroid is its own build again (Oct- + PATCH cycles DX7 FM → Juno-60 → Moog)
  • the keyboard now shows up as "SMK-37 Oscuro USB" over USB and "SMK-37 Oscuro BLE" over Bluetooth (on a Mac, remove the old device in Audio MIDI Setup once to see the new name)
  • test builds send their Bluetooth log live over USB (tools/ks_log.py); the Bluetooth lab diagnostics of 0.29.1-0.29.23 (start-up journal, hang reports, watchdog) are folded into this release
  • EDIT page sections now wrap as a carousel - '>' on the last section returns to the first, '<' on the first goes to the last

Fixed 8

  • USB Audio In keeps its clock correction when the unit's sample rate is a thousand ppm off the computer's.
  • USB Audio In starved the ring (heavy distortion); the unit now also checks the OUT endpoint between frame interrupts and reports how many packets each path found.
  • a sequencer warning (such as "Outside pattern length") no longer switches the metronome setting
  • in the sequencer, Right after Sequence Tools goes back to the lanes instead of wandering into Fader Setup and Split Zones; lane names fit their pads; the STEPS hint fits the screen
  • moving a fader shows the fader screen again, and a fader set up in Globe → Synthesizer → Fader Setup moves its synth parameter across the parameter's whole range
  • updating from 0.30 or older keeps your Globe settings (active preset, scales, clock source, screen colour, synth on/off) instead of resetting them
  • Scale Fix, Chord Voicing, Key Off, the metronome volume and fader assignments are remembered after power-off again (they were never saved since 0.31)
  • Bluetooth MIDI in the DX7 Bluetooth test build connects and plays: the start no longer hangs or freezes the screen, Bluetooth off disconnects the computer, and Bluetooth starts by itself at power-on when it was left on (hold Globe at power-on to skip it). Switching it on later fades the sound out for the radio's ~0.7 s set-up instead of freezing mid-note
