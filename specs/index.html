Specs

The details.

What Oscuro Viroid does and where its limits are, in one place.

Engine

Oscillators
Three, with sub, noise and ring modulator; sync and FM
Oscillator modes
Classic waves, Wavetable, Wave PWM, Grain, Formant
Filters
Two, in four routings, with saturation
Modulators
Four envelopes, three LFOs with tempo sync
Unison and HyperSaw
Up to six unison voices; up to nine saws per oscillator
Polyphony
Governed: depends on the patch, effects, modulation and clock speed
Modulation matrix
Six slots, forty sources, three destinations per slot, routes that modulate routes

Effects

Types
Twenty: twelve familiar and eight new
Rack
Up to six slots, as sends or inserts, in a choice of layouts
Audio block
64 samples by default, 128 for more headroom

Patches

Formats
Access Virus A, B, C and TI patches, with arpeggiator settings
Memory
Four banks of 128, stored compactly
Recall
From the keyboard, or Bank Select and Program Change over USB MIDI
Export
Any bank, saved to a file

Connections

USB MIDI
Notes, controllers and pressure in and out; DAW protocol on its own port if you split ports
USB audio
Stereo 48 kHz sound card: record the synth, or play your computer's audio through the headphones; gain from -12 to +6 dB
MIDI clock
Sends clock, start, stop and continue; following an incoming clock is in testing
Name on your computer
SMK-37 Oscuro USB, with its own USB identity

Performance

Processor
360 MHz stock; 384 to 420 MHz as an option
Scan speed
Auto, or 8 to 24 kHz in half-kilohertz steps
Voice limits
Max Voices, HyperSaw cap and cut order, Audio Block

Fits

Keyboards
M-Vave SMK-37 Elite; tested on the SMK-37 Pro, Donner Starrykey-37PLAY and EROK MKE-P37
Display
The keyboard's own 240 by 240 pixel screen

Leaves out

Bluetooth
Not in this build
Step sequencer
Not in this build; its memory goes to the synth and the effects

Acknowledgements

Oscuro's effects were written for this firmware. Where an idea came from somebody else's work, it is named here. No source code was copied into the effects below.

  • Sinew and PowerSag are re-implemented after the ideas of the Airwindows plugins of the same names, by Chris Johnson (MIT licence), with different internals.
  • OTT follows the well-known upward-and-downward multiband compression idea, studied through the open OTT implementations by Rui-727, Sakhnovkrg (vitOTTx) and Tsarpf. The code here was written from the algorithm, not taken from them.
  • Hyper and Dimension borrow their controls and idea from the effects of the same names in Serum, by Xfer Records. They are our own implementations, not copies. Fold is a small wavefolder in the spirit of the one in DaisySP (MIT licence), written from scratch.
  • Fract Res, Morph and the other effects are our own designs. Virus is a trademark of Access Music; Oscuro is not affiliated with or endorsed by Access, Xfer, Airwindows or any other name mentioned here.
